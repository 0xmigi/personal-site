Eval is a staking/escrow marketplace that attacks the job market's broken incentives. Both sides put up stake — quality and conviction from applicants, a guaranteed response and categorical feedback from employers — with the guarantees enforced by escrow, on a crypto + agent-payments backend.

The thesis: the "job market" is a fog-of-war courtship frenzy, not a real market. You fix that with skin in the game.
