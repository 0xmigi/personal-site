Livestock is a launchpad for narrative tokens: short-lived tokens about one specific story around a public company, which expire into that company's tokenized stock. Bullish Tesla, buy TSLA. Bullish Tesla *and specifically the Austin robotaxi launch*, buy the `ROBOTAXI` narrative. If the story runs you end up holding more TSLA than the same dollars would have bought outright; if it doesn't, you still hold TSLA.

## why expiry

Memecoins make you find a greater fool before the music stops. Here there's a date on which the position becomes equity by default, so exit timing stops being the whole game. That one rule shapes the build: the market has to halt and the vault freeze at expiry, and you can't halt a Raydium pool you don't own, so the curve lives inside the program. It's a narrative market, not a prediction market — nothing checks whether the robotaxis actually launched; the payoff comes from flows, not truth. Pinocchio program on Solana, Token-2022 narrative mints, xStocks for the equity, Jupiter for the swap in.
