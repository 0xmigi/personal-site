Every program that reaches Solana mainnet flows through one native program — the BPF Upgradeable Loader. On Record watches that single chokepoint, strips out the copy-paste (exact-bytecode dupes, then near-dupe clusters like pump.fun and token forks), and ranks what survives by how *novel* it actually is. Every surface leads with an address, a slot, a signature — facts, deduped and scored, no LLM prose.

## why I'm building it

It answers a question no explorer and no crypto AI agent can today: *"show me the top novel programs deployed to Solana today."* That's a novelty-*definition* problem, not a data-fetch problem — and the definition is the product. It's also a working writeup of a Helius integration: the whole ingestion path runs on Helius webhooks / Laserstream plus RPC, watching the one loader address that sees every deploy, upgrade, and authority change on the network.
