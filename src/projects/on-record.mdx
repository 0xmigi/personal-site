Building full-stack on crypto, I keep needing the same thing and never having it: a straight answer to *"what's actually new on Solana today?"* Explorers show me volume and price; AI agents summarize what's already popular. Neither tells me which of the ~2,000 daily program deploys is genuinely novel versus the thousandth pump.fun or token clone. So I built the tool I wanted to open every morning.

On Record watches the one native program every deploy flows through — the BPF Upgradeable Loader — strips out the copy-paste (exact-bytecode dupes, then near-dupe clusters), and ranks what survives by how *novel* it actually is. Every surface leads with an address, a slot, a signature: facts, deduped and scored, no LLM prose. It's a novelty-*definition* problem, not a data-fetch one — and the definition is the product. Under the hood it's also a working writeup of a Helius integration: the whole ingestion path runs on Helius webhooks / Laserstream plus RPC.
