An early experiment in onchain identity: a soulbound — non-transferable — KYC credential gated by [Worldcoin](https://worldcoin.org) proof-of-personhood. Prove you're a unique human once, carry the attestation onchain, and let apps check the credential instead of re-verifying you.

Built in 2022, it's an early thread of the user-owned identity ideas that later became [more physical and more social](/writing/more-physical-and-more-social).
